How to tell them apart

  • Parser. The date has its day and month swapped, whatever channel it came in by. The tenant writes day first; the shared parser reads month first. Fixing the parser fixes these.
  • booking-v3 hint. Also a swap, but only from tenants on booking-v3, and only through its form. The form’s hint asks for MM/DD/YYYY, people follow it, and the form reads their date day first. Fixing the parser does not fix these: the hint is the bug.
  • One-day move. Not a swap. The day moves by one (Tuesday shows as Monday), or two people in different time zones get different days for one meeting. The desk had already noted these reports; the cause table files them under the parser by tenant, so they had no ticket.

Why one ticket hid three

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. Reporters write the same few subjects for all three problems, so subject matching put them in one place. Intake’s tag does not help either: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the cause depends on the tenant and the form.
